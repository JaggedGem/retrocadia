The future, briefly

The Dreamcast arrived with a modem, a sculpted controller and an insistence that home consoles could feel like an arcade cabinet. Its VMU memory card added a tiny screen to the handshake between player and machine.

Its short commercial life only sharpened the legend. Soulcalibur, Jet Set Radio and Shenmue still feel like artifacts from a future that took a different route.

It was the first console where going online felt less like a feature and more like a doorway.

A cult object with a startup chime. Archive note, north gallery
