The archive goes portable

The Game Boy Advance carried a surprising amount of 16-bit feeling in a small, durable frame. Its horizontal shape and shoulder buttons made familiar genres feel newly comfortable in the hand.

It became a bridge between generations, bringing Metroid Fusion, Advance Wars and a deep catalogue of classic ports into backpacks everywhere. The screen was small; the excuses to keep playing were endless.

For many people it was the last console they truly carried with them.
