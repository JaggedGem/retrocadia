The room gets louder

Nintendo 64 hardware made 3D play feel immediate and social. Its unusual controller put an analog stick at the centre, while the four controller ports invited a whole room to take part.

Few exhibits capture the energy of Mario Kart 64 or GoldenEye 007: the pause between rounds, the accusations and one more match before dinner. Split-screen was not a compromise here; it was the entire point.

Cartridges kept load times short, which kept the arguments flowing.

Four ports changed the architecture of play. Visitor label, west gallery
