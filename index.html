The eras, logged

Four checkpoints in the living-room arcade.

  1. 1983 / The resetDedicated home hardware finds its second act, trading novelty for a new kind of neighbourhood ritual.
  2. 1990 / 16-bit glowColour, sound and character design become bold enough to live in memory long after the power switch clicks off.
  3. 1994 / The polygon arrivesOptical media opens the door to cinematic worlds, full-motion experiments and a new vocabulary of 3D play.
  4. 2000 / The networked roomOnline play begins moving the social centre of gaming from one couch toward an entire connected world.
